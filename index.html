Selected project / 01January 2026

Computer vision × communication

Hand2Voice.

A sign-language assistance prototype connecting hand inputs to text and speech.

  • MediaPipe Hands detects hand landmarks; OpenCV processes webcam frames.
  • wordfreq supports word prediction, while gTTS turns generated text into speech.
  • A Streamlit interface brings the workflow together.

Selected project / 02March 2026

Local language models × NLP

Nova.

A conversational AI prototype for sensitive support conversations and text analysis.

  • FastAPI connects the application with Phi-3 running through Ollama.
  • MongoDB stores conversation data.
  • DistilRoBERTa and spaCy named entity recognition extract text signals and entities for review.
